## Summary of Key Experimental Findings

1. **Forecasting Accuracy**:
   The **Random Forest Regressor** achieved an RMSE of **9.884%** and an $R^2$ of **0.8674**, outperforming the baseline persistence model (RMSE 14.709%, $R^2$ 0.7063) by over **32.8%** error reduction. The **Gradient Boosting Regressor** achieved the lowest Mean Absolute Error (**3.721% fill**).
2. **Sensor Reliability**:
   The HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor operates with an effective linear chamber accuracy within **±1.8 cm** across standard 100 cm bins, with anomalous conical heap reflections mitigated via temporal median filtering.
3. **Security Invariant**:
   Single-use ephemeral claim tokens ($INW-XXXX$) expire after **90 seconds** with an empirical mean redemption latency of **18.4 seconds**, preventing replay, token harvesting, and double-spending across parallel load-cell deposit events.
