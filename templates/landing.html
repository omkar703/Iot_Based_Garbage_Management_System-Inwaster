The Engineered Deposit Workflow

A synchronized sequence between ESP32 hardware, sensor validation, and backend security.

1

Capacity & Weighing

Ultrasonic sensors verify bin capacity while high-precision load cells measure stable waste weight.

2

Safe Gate Operation

Upon backend approval, the servo mechanism opens for safe disposal and returns to the closed position.

3

Short-Lived OLED QR

An ephemeral 90-second cryptographic token renders as a QR code directly on the dustbin's OLED display.

4

Points & Vouchers

Scan via camera to credit your auditable points ledger and redeem real discounts from partner businesses.
